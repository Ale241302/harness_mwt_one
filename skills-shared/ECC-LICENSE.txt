Third-party content in this directory
=====================================

Everything under `skills-shared/` except `interview-me/` is vendored from ECC
(https://github.com/affaan-m/ECC), version 2.2.2, by Affaan Mustafa, under the

  - 165 skills, copied verbatim; the curated ones stay model-invocable and the rest carry `disable-model-invocation: true` (user-invocable via `/name`).

crypto, and Apple/mobile stacks) were pruned to keep the catalog to business and
